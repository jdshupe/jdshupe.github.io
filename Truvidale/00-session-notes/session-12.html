Summary

In this session, Thaldor Coldforce alerted his companions to the presence of Seiluna students on campus, prompting a search for their whereabouts. One student was apprehended at the Temple after being put to sleep by one of the party members, while Thaldor and Orianna managed another student outside the Wizard Towers. The evening concluded with a mysterious package addressed to Ren, which left Alice visibly shaken and led her to retreat to her rooms, raising concerns among her friends.

Journal Entry

Thaldor very quickly realized that the Seiluna students had moved through campus and were most likely up to no good. How he assumed as much, I do not know, but I do not question the man, he has more than earned my trust at this point. I do hope I have has.

Heeding his call, I ran out the front doors of the Student Commons, followed closely by Orianna, and scanned for any signs of the other students. I spotted a set of tracks heading off to the far side of campus and Orianna thought she saw a set heading into the Colosseum (I don't think there were but I let her do her). We decided to split up and head our own ways.

As it was my own experience I will cover my own findings first.

Following the tracks I saw going across campus brought me to the Bestiary Stables. As I approached, I figure the best way to find out what was going on was to blend in so I cast Disguise Self to appear like one of the rich brats we just beat. Not seeing anyone immediately around I yelled out to see if anyone was nearby and heard a response from the Temple up the hill.

As I ran up and entered the temple I saw 2 students; one larger one acting as a body guard and one smaller, knelt next to the shrine to Mystra. I figured I should get rid of the guard as I didn't want to have to kill these students, they were both children. I looked to the larger one and said "Syvis wants to see you now." to which he promptly obeyed. He is either far too trusting or far too dumb, oh well, my gain.

With him out of the way I walked over and knelt by the smaller one. They seemed to be reading the plaque at the bottom of the shrine and writing notes in their journal. I remained close by while they finished up their work and then stood to leave. As they did I asked to see the journal just to know what we were reporting, they decided to be a good student and give it to Syvis first, which obviously couldn't happen. With some quicky thinking and a well applied charm I put the student to sleep. I grabbed from them the notebook, some gold they had in their pockets and payment for the nice treatment, and their boot as you can never have too many boots.

I decided to continue the loop about campus to make sure that any other renegade students were taken care of and ran into Orianna and Thaldor outside the Wizard Towers with another student. They seemed to have this one well under control but I did go ahead and cast another sleep spell on them as well to just make the whole situation simpler. The three of us then headed back to the Student Commons.

Upon arrival Thaldor sorted out some weird situation going on with Alice and Fenna that looked a little suspect but I will leave them to their business. He then confronted Syvis and had her and her students leave the campus and told them how upset he was about the whole situation. As they left I believe Orianna grabbed a note or book from one of them. I will have to ask her about that soon.

The next couple months were a bit of a blur, school resumed as normal, like we had never left. It was nice for things to just go back to how they were, class, studies, activities. During this time I found some time to talk with Trevalia about potions and expressed some interest in more advanced studies and experiments of my own. She led me to the 2nd underground floor of the Wizard Towers and gave me the key to an old unused lab there. It was set up with some rudimentary equipment and reagent and she said I am always welcome to talk to her about simpler items I may need.

She did also mention there is a storeroom for more valuable and dangerous reagents that I can talk to her or Thaldor about if I am looking for something. I will have to remember this and maybe do some looking of my own.

I will write an entry for today as the circumstances seem to warrant it. As we all went into the Student Commons for dinner, Thaldor brought over some mail for Alice. It looked like a fairly normal package but I did notice it was addressed to a Ren, not Alice. She seemed to pale upon seeing it and excused herself to go back to her rooms. I didn't think too much of it but she did not come out of her rooms that evening. I talked with Fenna who assured us that Alice was okay but I will continue to make entries as the situation unfolds.

Key Learnings

  • Seiluna student's were trying to discover sources of magic on campus for Syvis
  • Trevalia gave me a key to a lab in the second basement of the Wizard Towers
  • There is a storeroom of power magic items somewhere on campus that Trevalia and Thaldor have access to.
